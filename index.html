Our Story

A neighborhood gem since 2018

Nestled on a tree-lined street in historic Brooklyn Heights, Vineapple offers an escape from the city bustle. Our light-filled space opens onto a private garden courtyard—perfect for morning coffee, lazy brunches, and intimate conversations.

We source our ingredients from local farms and our flowers from Brooklyn's finest growers. Every dish is made with care, every cup brewed with intention.
